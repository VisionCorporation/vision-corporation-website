Who we are

Africa should design and build more of its own technology.

We are a team of entrepreneurs who share one belief: Africa should design and build more of its own technology. Too much of the continent's infrastructure depends on systems made somewhere else, for someone else's conditions. We want to change that, one practical project at a time.

Our inspiration

We draw our inspiration from a symbol. Uac Nkanea is the Adinkra symbol for technological advancement, and it reminds us of the spirit of innovation and progress we want to carry into everything we build.

What we do today

We begin with software.

It is the fastest way to deliver value and to fund the harder work ahead. Today, we are a web design and development agency, building for startups and growing businesses. We also help them get found and grow.

Our vision

To be the enduring force that powers Africa's technological renaissance, with integrated hardware, software and AI that deliver reliable energy and efficient transportation for generations to come.

Our mission

To design, build and scale practical, locally led solutions to Africa's most critical infrastructure challenges. We begin with software excellence, which funds and speeds up our work on integrated circuits, control systems and AI-enabled platforms, so that the impact outlasts any single project.
